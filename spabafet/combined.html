3. Student Organizations & Leadership

Student Organizations

Clubs and societies promote leadership, skills and community life. Current recognized groups:

  • Student Council
  • Drama & Media Club
  • Science Society
  • Entrepreneurship Club
  • Environmental Club
  • Religious Fellowship Groups

Leadership & Elections

Elections for Student Council occur every academic year. Roles include: President, VP, Secretary, Treasurer, Faculty Reps.

Nominations close two weeks before elections. The election committee will verify eligibility.
